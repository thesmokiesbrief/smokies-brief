    National park

      Good to know

      • Parking in the national park for more than 15 minutes needs a parking tag: $5 a day or $15 a week. Buy one at a visitor center, or online at recreation.gov and print it (details).
      • Never leave food, coolers or trash outside. Bears are common around cabins.
      • Mountain roads and steep driveways ice up fast in cold weather. Drive slowly and use low gear downhill.
