Short-term rental rules

Sevier County short-term rental rules, by area

The same cabin can face different rules depending on which side of a city line it sits on. Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge and Sevierville each run their own permits. Everything outside city limits answers to Sevier County. Find your area, then check the details.

Which rules apply to your cabin?

  1. Check the city line, not the mailing address. A Sevierville or Pigeon Forge mailing address can sit outside city limits. Sevierville has a city limits verification map.
  2. Inside a city: follow that city's permit rules, get a city business license and a county business license, and pay lodging tax to the city. Sevier County Clerk
  3. Outside city limits: get the county's yearly Fire Marshal permit, a county business license, and pay lodging tax to the County Trustee.
  4. Buying a rental? Ask whether the current permit transfers to you. In Pigeon Forge's R-1 zone, it doesn't.
