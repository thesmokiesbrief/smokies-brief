Short-term rental rules

Pigeon Forge short-term rental rules: R-1 permit, occupancy and taxes

Pigeon Forge's biggest rule is about the R-1 low-density residential zone: short-term rentals there are allowed only for properties that were already renting by August 13, 2018, and they need a city operating permit. Outside R-1, zoning decides what's allowed.

Facts checked against official sources on November 17, 2026. Meeting agendas checked every morning.

PermitShort-Term Rental Unit Operating Permit (R-1 zone)
Cost$300 application, renewed every year
InspectionCovered by the permit fee
RenewalEvery year, at least 14 business days before it expires

What Pigeon Forge requires

The R-1 cutoff
In the R-1 district, only owners who were renting short term on or before August 13, 2018, and who paid rental taxes for at least six of the twelve months before that date, can use the property as a short-term rental. Source
Buying an R-1 rental
The city can revoke the permit when the owner stops owning the property, so a buyer can't count on taking it over. Get the city's answer in writing before you buy. Source
Permit types
Owner occupied, non-owner occupied, and unoccupied. Source
Cost and term
$300 to apply, not refundable. The permit is good for one calendar year, and the renewal fee is due at least 14 business days before it expires. Source
What the application needs
A signed life safety affidavit, a 24-hour local contact, a site plan and floor plan showing parking, a valid business license, and proof that rental taxes were paid. Source
Occupancy
No more than two guests per bedroom plus two. Source
Advertising
Every ad, online or off, has to show the operating permit number. Source
Revocation
The city can revoke a permit for false information, a safety threat, a change of ownership, 30 months without rental use, or three violations. Source
Taxes
Operators owe the city hotel occupancy privilege tax, local sales tax and gross receipts tax, plus Tennessee sales tax and gross receipts tax. Source
Outside R-1
The zoning ordinance lists overnight and weekly rentals as uses in some commercial districts. Check the zoning of any address with the city before you buy. City Hall: (865) 453-9061. Source

These facts come from the city's 2019 zoning ordinance update. The city may have amended it since, so confirm with City Hall.
