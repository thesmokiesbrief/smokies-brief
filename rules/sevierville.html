Sevierville short-term rental rules: operational permit and inspection

Inside Sevierville city limits, a short-term rental needs an operational permit before it can operate or advertise. The permit is renewed every year and comes with a fire department life safety inspection.

Facts checked against official sources on November 17, 2026. Meeting agendas checked every morning.

PermitShort-Term Rental Operational Permit (city planning and fire department)
CostAnnual fee, set by the city
InspectionYes, every year
RenewalEvery year, before the old permit expires

What Sevierville requires

Who needs it
Every short-term rental inside Sevierville city limits, before it operates or advertises. Use the city limits map to check an address. Source
How to apply
Apply online through MyGovernmentOnline (MGO). You pay the annual fee and provide proof of insurance, and the fire department schedules the inspection. Source
Neighbor notice in MDR zoning
In the Medium Density Residential district, the city notifies every property within 100 feet. Apply at least 21 days before the next Planning Commission meeting. Neighbors get seven days to respond, and opposition sends it to a public hearing. Source
Posting
Post the permit inside the main entrance, and a short form of it where it can be seen from the front of the rental. Source
Revocation
Three citations or violations within one permit year revoke the permit for a year. During that year, only an unrelated new owner can get a new one. Source
Sprinklers
A fire sprinkler system is required for any rental that sleeps more than 12. Rentals more than three stories above grade must meet the International Building Code. Source
Fire safety items
Interconnected smoke alarms on every level and in every sleeping area, a 5 lb 2A:10BC extinguisher on every level that is tagged every year, an escape window or door in every sleeping room, and a street number at least 4 inches tall that can be seen from the street. Source
Questions
Sevierville Fire Department, (865) 868-1585. Source
