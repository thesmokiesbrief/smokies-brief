Sevier County short-term rental rules outside city limits

Cabins outside any city limits, including much of Wears Valley and the county's back roads, answer to Sevier County. Since January 1, 2024, every one of them needs a yearly permit and inspection from the county Fire Marshal's Office.

Facts checked against official sources on November 17, 2026. Meeting agendas checked every morning.

What Unincorporated Sevier County requires

Who needs it
Every short-term rental in Sevier County outside city limits. One application per address. Source
Started
January 1, 2024. The county extended the first application deadline to June 30, 2024. Source
Cost
$250 a year for a rental that sleeps 12 or fewer. Over 12, it's $250 plus $25 for each additional guest. Payments aren't refundable. Source
How to apply
Online through the county's Community Connect portal: register as a business owner, add each rental address, choose Permits, then Apply for a Permit and attach the completed application. Source
Inspection
The permit is issued after the inspection. Follow-up inspections happen every year in the month your first permit was issued. A third inspection, and any after it, costs $25 each. Source
What inspectors check
Smoke alarms, carbon monoxide detectors, escape routes, electrical safety and heating and cooling. The county asks for a 2A10BC fire extinguisher on each level. Source
Business license and lodging tax
Get a Sevier County business license from the County Clerk and pay lodging tax to the County Trustee. Source
Trash from cleaning crews
Short-term rental cleaning companies must take rental trash to the Center View Road multipurpose convenience center and pay a disposal fee, not the residential centers. Source
Questions
Sevier County Fire Marshal's Office, (865) 774-3603. Source
