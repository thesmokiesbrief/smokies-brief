Gatlinburg short-term rental rules: Tourist Residency Permit

Inside Gatlinburg city limits, an overnight rental needs a Tourist Residency Permit from the city, and the property has to be zoned for overnight rental use. Check zoning before you buy or apply.

Facts checked against official sources on November 17, 2026. Meeting agendas checked every morning.

What Gatlinburg requires

Who needs it
Anyone using a property inside Gatlinburg city limits for overnight rentals, as long as the property is zoned for it. Source
Zoning comes first
The city says it's the applicant's job to confirm with the Building and Planning Department that the property is zoned for overnight rental use before applying. Call (865) 436-7792. Source
Cost
$200 for a unit with 2 or fewer bedrooms, plus $75 for each bedroom over 2. The fee pays for the fire and building inspection. Source
What the application asks for
Owner name and contact, the rental's location and name, number of bedrooms and stories, maximum occupancy, gross square footage, and the rental agent's name and contact. Source
Where to apply
Drop the application and payment at the city's Customer Service Center or Finance Department, or mail it to the city. Source
Business licenses and lodging tax
Rentals inside city limits need a city business license in addition to a Sevier County business license, and pay lodging tax to the city. Source
Bear-resistant trash cans
Properties holding a Tourist Residency Permit are inside the city's animal-resistant container zone. Source
Questions
City of Gatlinburg, (865) 436-1400. Source
