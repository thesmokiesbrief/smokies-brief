Sevier County cabin market report

Home values, sales, supply and nightly rates for Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Sevierville and the rest of Sevier County. New numbers arrive each month when Zillow publishes them, and every month is saved below.

Latest numbers: October 2026. Page checked November 17, 2026.

The typical higher-priced home in Sevier County was worth $680,492 in October 2026, down 2.8% from a year earlier. There were 2,456 homes for sale, up 8% from a year earlier, and about 9.1 months of supply. Zillow's market heat index read 25, which points to a buyer's market.
