Privacy

Your privacy on The Smokies Brief

Short version: we only collect your email if you sign up, we use it only to send what you asked for, and we never sell it. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking scripts on this site.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Who we are

The Smokies Brief is published by The Smokies Brief by Weak Things, LLC, PO Box 115, Alcoa, TN 37701. Questions about this page: hello@thesmokiesbrief.com.

Emails you sign up for

  • When you sign up for the Monday Brief or big weekend alerts, your email address is stored by Kit, the service that sends our emails.
  • We use it only to send the emails you chose. We don't sell, rent or share it with anyone else.
  • Like most email services, Kit records whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. We look at those totals to see what's useful.

Using the site

  • No tracking. We don't use analytics, ad pixels or tracking cookies.
  • Power checker. Addresses you type are looked up with free public geocoders (the U.S. Census, with Photon as a backup) to place them on the map. This site doesn't save them.
  • Checklists. Your checkmarks are saved in a small cookie on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Outside services. Fonts (Fontshare), maps (OpenFreeMap), chart and map code (jsDelivr, unpkg), park webcam images (National Park Service) and videos (YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode, only after you press play) load from their own servers. Like any website, they can see your IP address.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Links. Headlines and other links take you to their original publishers, whose own privacy policies apply.

Children

The Smokies Brief is written for property owners and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
