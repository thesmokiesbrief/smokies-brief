    Market Report · October 2026

    Sevier County cabin market

    Updated monthly Next update mid-December

    In plain terms: buyer's market. Higher-priced homes are down 23% from their July 2022 peak, while lower-priced homes are up 4.4% over the past year. Homes are taking longer to sell, with 9.1 months of supply (6 is balanced).

    Buyer's or seller's market?

    Favors buyersFavors sellers
    25Buyer's market

    Peaked at 82 in Sept 2020. Zillow's market heat index; 50 is balanced.

    What it means: more homes than buyers. A good time to buy and negotiate; slower to sell.

    The luxury vs. entry-level gap

    Top third of homesBottom third
    −23%Top third since its July 2022 peak ($881K → $680K)
    +4.4%Bottom third, past 12 months ($266K)

    What it means: higher-priced homes have lost the most ground. Lower-priced homes are holding their value better.

    Months of supply

    9.1 months

    Homes for sale ÷ monthly sales. Under 6 favors sellers, over 6 favors buyers. Was 2.1 in Oct 2021.

    What it means: if you list now, expect a longer wait and more room for buyers to negotiate.
